Decarb-AI: AI-Powered Pathways to Climate Resilience

About Decarb-AI

The first Innovate for Ireland national centre, 'Decarb-AI: AI-Powered Pathways to Climate Resilience' represents a major milestone for the Innovate for Ireland programme.

Created in partnership with AIB and Research Ireland, the €5.7m Decarb-AI centre will aim to harness the power of artificial intelligence (AI) to accelerate Ireland’s transition to a climate-resilient, low-carbon future. By combining advanced AI research with real-world climate challenges, Decarb-AI has the potential to generate solutions that are both scientifically rigorous and nationally impactful.

Research Focus Areas

The iScholars’ research at Decarb-AI research centre will focus on using cutting-edge AI to advance climate mitigation and adaptation across Ireland. Key focus areas include:

Energy & Infrastructure

AI-optimised renewable energy systems and datacentre sustainability.

Environment & Ecology

Machine learning for water quality forecasting and peatland restoration.

Land Use

Earth-observation and biodiversity modelling for land-use policy.

Sustainable Finance

AI-supported sustainable finance tools for SMEs.

Decision Support

Transparent AI decision-support systems for real-time decarbonisation planning.

Our Team

Through Decarb-AI, we are fostering a research environment where advanced data science meets urgent environmental necessity. The centre operates across highly regarded Irish higher education institutions, including University College Dublin (lead institution), Trinity College Dublin, Dublin City University, Technological University Dublin, University of Limerick, University of Galway (via ICHEC), and University College Cork.

Join the Programme

Decarb-AI will welcome 30 iScholars across three intakes. All of these iScholars will undertake fully-funded, four-year PhDs under the supervision of leading academic researchers.

Applications for a second cohort of researchers to Decarb-AI will be invited from ambitious candidates with backgrounds in AI, data science, engineering, environmental science, ecology, geography, finance, and related fields who want to build high-impact AI solutions for climate resilience.
