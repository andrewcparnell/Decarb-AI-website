How the centre works

The magic is in the mix

iScholars don’t work in isolation. The cohort, their supervisors, our institutions and frontier AI combine to turn separate PhDs into a national centre.

The cohortiScholars train together, starting with a three-month programme in AI, decarbonisation and high-performance computing, and keep learning from each other across disciplines and institutions.
SupervisorsLeading academics from every partner institution guide each project, with many co-supervised across disciplines and universities.
InstitutionsSeven partners, from universities to ICHEC, Ireland’s national high-performance computing centre, open up their expertise, facilities and networks to every iScholar.
Frontier AIFrom AI weather models like ECMWF’s Anemoi to physics-informed neural networks and large language models, applied to real climate problems.

Research themes in action
THEME 01

Energy resources

AI-optimised renewable energy systems and datacentre sustainability.

Machine learning for load balancing, grid integration of wind and solar, and the thermal management of national data infrastructure: forecasting grid surges and cutting curtailment losses.

  • Load forecasting
  • Grid integration
  • Offshore wind
  • Data-centre cooling
THEME 02

Water security

Machine learning for water quality forecasting and flood and drought risk.

Spatio-temporal AI monitors catchment health, predicts runoff, and forecasts compounding floods and droughts weeks to months ahead, giving early warning to protect water supplies.

  • Water quality
  • Floods & droughts
  • Sensor networks
  • Early warning
THEME 03

Land management

Peatland restoration, earth observation and biodiversity for land-use policy.

Satellite remote sensing and computer vision track land cover, forest carbon and biodiversity, giving objective spatial intelligence for agricultural policy and habitat restoration.

  • Earth observation
  • Computer vision
  • Peatlands
  • Biodiversity
THEME 04

Emissions measurement

Sensors and physics-informed AI for greenhouse gas accounting.

Physics-informed AI links local sensors, from root cameras to soil probes, to ecosystem-scale greenhouse gas fluxes, so Ireland can measure, verify and reduce its emissions.

  • GHG fluxes
  • Physics-informed AI
  • Carbon accounting
  • Sensors
THEME 05

Sustainable finance

AI-supported sustainable finance and climate risk tools for SMEs.

NLP and machine learning pipelines automate ESG risk assessment, green portfolio alignment and transition-risk scoring, helping capital flow to verifiable decarbonisation.

  • NLP
  • ESG risk
  • Climate risk
  • SMEs

Energy resources

Chantelle Amoako-Atta

University College Dublin

AI for climate-resilient offshore wind in Ireland

Extending ECMWF’s Anemoi AI forecasting system to predict wind extremes in Irish waters, and linking them to structural fatigue models so offshore wind farms stay reliable as the climate changes.
