01 · About

About Decarb-AI

Decarb-AI: AI-Powered Pathways to Climate Resilience is the first Innovate for Ireland National Centre, bringing together exceptional talent, ambitious industry partners and a future-focused nation to accelerate Ireland’s transition to a climate-resilient, low-carbon future.

Decarb-AI: the first Innovate for Ireland National Centre, uniting seven Irish institutions around AI for climate.

A major milestone for Innovate for Ireland

Established in partnership with AIB and Research Ireland, the Decarb-AI Centre aims to harness the power of artificial intelligence to accelerate Ireland’s transition to a climate-resilient, low-carbon future.

Researchers, industry and government each bring something different, and the Centre is where they meet.

01 · The programme

A global scholarship programme

Innovate for Ireland brings together exceptional talent, ambitious industry partners, and a future-focused nation to tackle some of the world’s greatest challenges. Unique among international scholarship programmes, it supports advanced PhD research focused on sustainability, innovation, and societal impact.

02 · The partnership

Government and industry, side by side

Established through a partnership between the Irish Government and the private sector, the programme takes a progressive approach to doctoral education, attracting, developing, and retaining world-class PhD researchers across higher education institutions throughout the island of Ireland.

03 · The iScholars

Challenge-based research

Our researchers, known as iScholars, undertake challenge-based research aligned with national priority areas, with a strong emphasis on sustainability, innovation, and entrepreneurial leadership to help address the world’s most pressing issues.

04 · The impact

Rigorous, relevant, real-world

By combining cutting-edge AI research with real-world climate challenges, Decarb-AI seeks to develop solutions that are scientifically rigorous, commercially relevant, and capable of delivering meaningful national and global impact.

02 · Research

Research Areas

The iScholars’ research at the Decarb-AI research centre focuses on using cutting-edge AI to advance climate mitigation and adaptation across Ireland. Pick a theme to explore what we work on and who is working on it.

Energy & Infrastructure

AI-optimised renewable energy systems and datacentre sustainability.

Research within this stream investigates machine learning approaches for dynamic load balancing, grid integration of intermittent renewable sources (wind, solar), and thermal management systems for national data infrastructure. iScholars focus on high-dimensional predictive modelling to forecast grid surges and minimise curtailment losses across Ireland's energy network.

  • Load forecasting
  • Grid integration
  • Wind & solar
  • Curtailment
  • Data-centre cooling
Some of the people in this area

Environment & Ecology

Machine learning frameworks for water quality forecasting and peatland restoration.

This focus area leverages spatio-temporal AI models to monitor hydrological catchment health, predict agricultural runoff patterns, and evaluate carbon sequestration trajectories in restored peatlands. By integrating sensor networks with deep learning, researchers develop early-warning tools for ecological degradation and water source protection.

  • Water quality
  • Sensor networks
  • Peatland carbon
  • Ecosystem stability
  • Early warning
Some of the people in this area

Land Use Systems

Coupling earth-observation arrays and biodiversity modelling for data-driven land-use policy.

Projects here synthesise high-resolution satellite remote sensing (Sentinel/Copernicus arrays) with computer vision to track real-time land cover changes, forestry carbon stocks, and ecosystem biodiversity metrics. The goal is to provide granular, objective spatial intelligence to guide regional agricultural policy and habitat restoration.

  • Earth observation
  • Computer vision
  • Forest carbon
  • Land cover
  • Biodiversity
Some of the people in this area

Sustainable Finance

Designing sophisticated, AI-supported sustainable finance appraisal tools for SMEs.

This stream addresses the data bottleneck in climate finance by creating NLP and machine learning pipelines that automate ESG risk assessments, green portfolio alignment, and transition risk scoring for small-to-medium enterprises. The research helps financial institutions deploy targeted capital toward verifiable decarbonisation efforts.

  • NLP
  • ESG risk
  • Transition risk
  • Green portfolios
  • SME appraisal
Some of the people in this area

Decision Support

Developing transparent, explainable AI decision-support systems for real-time decarbonisation planning.

Focusing on Explainable AI (XAI) and multi-objective optimisation, this stream translates complex climate models into interpretable, interactive tools for local authorities and policymakers. Scholars design robust frameworks that balance socio-economic impacts, infrastructure costs, and emissions targets under uncertain climate scenarios.

  • Explainable AI
  • Optimisation
  • Uncertainty
  • Climate policy
  • HPC

04 · Join

Join the Programme

Decarb-AI will welcome 30 iScholars across three distinct recruitment intakes. Every researcher undertakes a fully-funded, four-year PhD under the guidance of established academic experts.

Applications for our subsequent cohort of researchers are open to candidates displaying excellent quantitative backgrounds in AI, data science, engineering, environmental science, ecology, geography, finance, or related mathematical disciplines who aim to build high-impact computational tools for global climate resilience.

Each iScholar receives an annual stipend of €31,000 with all university research fees covered. iScholars also receive a generous travel and computing allowance.

All iScholars undertake a three-month training scheme on AI, decarbonisation, and high-performance computing that runs from September to December at University College Dublin.
